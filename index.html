      Welcome to Easton Roofing

      Where Integrity Matters

      Easton Roofing Company is a full-service roofing, gutter and attic insulation specialist based in Prairie Village, KS. We provide Commercial and Residential roofing, commercial roofing and attic insulation services within the Kansas City metro area and Johnson County, as well as Lawrence, KS. We are committed to using the best products on the market installed by the best roofers in the industry.
      Whether you need a simple gutter repair or a full roof replacement, you’ve come to the right place.

      How Can We Help You?

      Easton Roofing specializes in the services your home needs to keep you and your family comfortable, safe and secure. We offer the following:

      • Residential roof replacement
      • Residential roof repair & maintenance
      • Storm damage & emergency roof repair
      • Attic insulation installation & improvement
      • Gutter installation & repair
      • New construction roofing

      The Easton Difference

      We know there are a lot of roofers in the Kansas City area. So, what sets Easton Roofing apart? Our many satisfied clients would tell you it’s our straightforward approach to customer service. Investing in a new roof, or improving an old roof, can be a stressful experience, especially when you work with a contractor that doesn’t really have your best interest at heart. When you choose Easton Roofing, we’ll never try to sell you something you don’t need, or surprise you with additional charges halfway through the project.
      We’ll fix your roof, gutters or attic — plain and simple. We’ll treat you like family and your home like it’s ours. If you want honest service and excellent workmanship, choose our company.

      Trust Easton Roofing

      We’re excited to work with you! Contact us today to get started on your project. Kansas City residents can contact us at (913) 933-8065 and homeowners in Lawrence can reach us at (785) 766-1259. You can also request a free consultation with one of our roofing specialists.
